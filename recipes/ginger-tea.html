Ginger Tea Recipe

Description:

Ginger Tea is a spicy, warming, and caffeine-free drink made from fresh or dried ginger root. It is widely consumed for its soothing properties, and many variations exist that include additions like lemon, honey, or other spices. .

Ingredients:

  • Fresh or dried ginger root
  • Water
  • Lemon (optional)
  • Honey (optional)
  • Other spices like cinnamon or cloves (optional)

Steps

  1. Peel and slice the ginger root.
  2. Add water to a pot and bring to a boil.
  3. Add the sliced ginger to the boiling water.
  4. Let it simmer for about 10-15 minutes.
  5. Strain the tea into a cup.
  6. Add lemon, honey, or other spices if desired.
  7. Stir well and serve hot.
  8. Enjoy your soothing ginger tea.
