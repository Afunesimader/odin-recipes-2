Scrambled Eggs Recipe

Description:

Scrambled Eggs is a dish of eggs prepared by beating them with a little liquid and then cooking and stirring gently.

Ingredients:

  • Eggs
  • Milk or cream
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Butter or oil

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add a splash of milk or cream.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Whisk the mixture until well combined.
  5. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add butter or oil.
  6. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet.
  7. Let it sit for a few seconds, then gently stir with a spatula.
  8. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the eggs are softly set.
  9. Remove from heat and serve immediately.
