Beef Samosa Recipe

Description:

Beef Samosa is a popular Indian snack consisting of a crispy pastry filled with spiced ground beef and vegetables, then deep-fried to golden perfection.

Ingredients:

  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Green chili
  • Cilantro
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, garam masala)
  • Salt
  • Pastry dough or samosa wrappers
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Prepare the filling by sautéing ground beef with chopped onion, garlic, ginger, green chili, cilantro, and spices until cooked through.
  2. Season with salt.
  3. Wrap the filling in pastry dough or samosa wrappers, sealing the edges.
  4. Heat oil in a deep fryer or large skillet to 350°F (175°C).
  5. Fry the samosas in batches until golden brown and crispy.
  6. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  7. Serve hot with chutney or sauce of your choice.
